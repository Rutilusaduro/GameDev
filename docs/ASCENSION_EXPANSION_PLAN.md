# Expansion Plan: 20 Ideas + The Ascension System

Design session output. Twenty ideas to radically expand Professor Sim:
two for character creation (Part A), three for UX (Part B), five for
midgame (Part C), and a ten-step implementation plan for the endgame
**Ascension system** (Part D) — every girl, at max size, transforms into a
supernatural form reborn at 100 lbs with new mechanics, abilities, events,
and dialogue. Part E is the full 18-girl form roster with per-girl event
chains that extend each girl's existing evolved-form arc.

House rules bind everything here: adults only, consensual fantasy, no
health-consequence framing, size desirable throughout, engine labels never
in prose.

---

## Part A — Character creation (2)

### A1. The Nineteenth Chair (player-built student)

One custom student, built at semester start from the systems the cast
already runs on. The creation flow is five screens: (1) archetype — pick
from the existing eighteen or three reserved "open" archetypes (`debate`,
`astronomy`, `rotc`) that ship with trait-keyed content but no persona
owner; (2) bodyType + starting weight (stage 0–2 only; she earns the rest);
(3) gain stance and psych axis starting tiers, spent from a point budget so
min-maxing costs something visible; (4) voice contract — pick 5 owned
phrases and 1 taboo topic from curated lists per archetype (freeform text
can't be persona-pooled or linted, so the lists ARE the interface); (5)
wardrobe identity, three garments with `fitLbs` seeded to her frame — and,
once Ascension ships, her eventual form from any not yet claimed this run.

Mechanically she is data, not code:

```js
{ id: 18, custom: true, name, archetype, bodyType, pronouns,
  lbs, gainStance, psych: {...}, voiceKit: { owned: [...], taboo },
  formId: 'selkie' /* chosen */, wardrobe: [...] }
```

Trait-keyed generics already cover her everywhere; creation assembles a
**starter persona kit** — 2 lines per corruption tier per major slot
(`wi.replyDialogue`, `eat.firstBite`, `shift.interior`, diary base),
generated by filling voice-kit phrases into per-archetype line templates
and registered via `registerModuleVariants` under `studentId: 18` at
weight 4, exactly like any `personas.js`. The Dossier (B1) tracks her like
anyone else; the Dialogue Lab gets her in its student dropdown for free
(it reads the roster). Lint: the sweep grid appends the custom student
when a save defines one, so her cells get the same coverage guarantee.
Payoff: replay identity, and proof the content architecture really is
data-driven — if the Nineteenth Chair needs special-case code anywhere,
that is a lint-worthy architecture bug. Explicit non-goal: no freeform
name-your-own-archetype; an archetype with zero keyed content would render
wildcard-only prose and read flat, which players would misread as a bug.

### A2. Origin decks (pickable backstories, replay divergence from week 1)

Each girl gets 2–3 origin cards shown at first meaningful contact, chosen
by the player and locked for the run. Examples across the cast: Brittany —
"ex-gymnast under a hard coach" (shame starts tier 2, defiance beats) vs
"pageant-circuit legacy" (obsession tier 2, mirror content early); Maya —
"moved every two years" (dependence climbs fast once earned) vs "eight
siblings, loud house" (food-as-territory beats, zero shame about seconds);
Chloé — "sent abroad after a scandal at home" vs "first of her family to
leave"; Serena — "washed out of nationals by two tenths" vs "walked away
undefeated and bored." A card sets: starting psych tiers (replacing the
flat defaults), ONE exclusive early event chain (2–3 beats, stages 0–3,
where the origin is visible in her behavior), one standing dialogue
register tweak, and a re-seeded beat 1 of her arc voice ladder.

Cost is bounded by construction: an origin is a `when` dimension —
`registerDimension('origin', (ctx) => ctx.subject?.origin ?? 'default')`
— plus roughly a dozen keyed variants per girl and the exclusive chain.
Origin-keyed variants pool WITH her existing content (specificity math
favors them when they apply), so nothing is rewritten. The card UI shows
mechanical effects plainly (this genre's players read stat lines happily)
plus one line of her voice per card so the pick feels like her, not like a
build. Two runs with different origin picks read differently at stage 0,
which is exactly where replays are currently most samey — and origins
compound with A1, since the custom girl picks an origin too. Coverage
rule: every origin-exclusive chain still satisfies stage coverage for its
band, and `origin: 'default'` must remain fully playable (lint sweeps it).

## Part B — UX (3)

### B1. The Dossier (per-girl living journal)

One screen per girl that turns save state into story. Layout, top to
bottom: **header strip** — portrait chip at current stage, name, week-
over-week delta badge; **the weight line** — full-run graph with rung-
crossing markers (tapping a marker replays that crossing's rendered scene
from a stored seed), garment-failure ticks along the x-axis, and her
`peakLbs` waterline once Ascension exists; **psych bands** — the four
axes drawn as stacked tier ribbons over time, so "her shame collapsed the
same week her obsession spiked" is visible as geometry; **pinned moments**
— cards generated from persistent flags ("the booth incident — week 9",
"first size up — week 5"), each opening its diary excerpt; **the diary**
— her existing diary entries inline, newest first; **wardrobe morgue** —
retired garments with cause of death ("waistband, week 11, honorable");
and front and center, the **next-threshold panel**: pounds to the next
rung, the garment closest to failure with its strain percentage, whichever
psych tier is within one event of shifting, and her next locked scene with
its unlock condition named in-fiction ("she isn't ready to be seen at the
gym yet").

Data cost is one weekly snapshot array per girl (`{week, lbs, psychTiers,
flags[]}` appended on week advance — a save-shape addition, version-
bumped) plus read-only assembly; nothing new is simulated. The genre's
engine is anticipation, and today that lives in the player's head. The
Dossier makes it a place you visit, and it doubles as the dev team's best
regression surface: any timeline that reads wrong is a state bug made
visible.

### B2. Scene stage (presentation layer for the text engine)

Scenes currently arrive as blocks. Present them as beats: paragraph-level
reveal on tap/keypress with a subtle settle animation (fast-forward on
hold; an instant-text toggle in settings for readers who hate drama), a
slim state header (locale glyph, week, her silhouette chip at current
stage — the chip alone quietly communicates "this scene knows how big she
is"), and choice buttons carrying intent icons — observe / press / feed /
comfort / wait — so menus read as stances rather than as a list of
sentences. A scrollback drawer holds the session transcript; long-press
any paragraph to pin it to the Dossier as a player-chosen moment.

Implementation is one renderer component consuming the beat structure
scenes already have (templates are already composed beat by beat; the
renderer just stops treating the output as one string). `opts.trace` tags
which spans came from which slot, which powers a dev overlay — tap a
sentence in dev builds, see `[wi.moodTag]`, flag it into the tuning loop
from inside the game. That closes the distance between playtesting and
the flag-batch protocol: the Dialogue Lab's reader tools become available
everywhere prose appears. Typography per DESIGN_BIBLE; motion respects
`prefers-reduced-motion`; the mobile build is the primary target since
Capacitor is already in the stack. Biggest perceived-quality win per line
of code available to this project.

### B3. Week planner + week-in-review

Planning: a slot board you drag girls onto. Each placed card previews the
slot's expected shape before you commit: predicted cost (her appetite
raises meal prices — the planner surfaces the exact number and its
week-over-week creep), scheduling conflicts (two girls, one venue, one
professor), synergy hints when a pairing has rivalry-web content (C1),
and interrupt likelihood drawn from real state ("Destiny is at hunger 3
and addiction 2 — expect a knock whether or not you plan her"). Unfilled
slots show what they'd default to instead of sitting blank, so skipping a
girl is a visible choice with a face on it.

Review: end of week, a short reel of delta cards — pounds gained per girl
with her reaction line rendered fresh, thresholds crossed (rung ceremonies
get full-bleed cards), garments lost, psych tier shifts, money in/out,
institution progress (C2) — each card tappable through to its Dossier
moment. The reel is also where slow weeks earn their keep: a week of
quiet maintenance still produces cards ("nothing changed, and she
noticed you noticed"), so the loop never pays out nothing. Closes the
promise the design bible makes: invest → change → SEE the change → plan
the next one. Dev note: the reel is assembled from the same weekly
snapshot the Dossier stores; one data source, two surfaces.

## Part C — Midgame (5)

### C1. Rivalry & pact webs (girl↔girl relationships)

A sparse girl-to-girl relation graph: each girl holds at most three edges,
typed **rival**, **partner-in-gain**, **mentor/protégée**, or **orbit**
(fascination without a name yet). Edges have intensity 0–3 and shift on
shared scenes, weigh-in deltas ("she passed her"), and player framing
choices. The engine was built for two-subject prose — `ctx.ref`,
`relSize`, `refArchetype` already exist — so edge scenes are ordinary
pools that happen to render with a ref: leaderboard needles between
Brittany and Serena that turn into joint training tables; Kylie filming
Tiffany's chapter feasts until the collab becomes its own show; Nadia
quietly profiling whoever gained fastest this month and mailing you the
memo; Priya turning anyone's milestone into a program with deliverables.

Seed each girl with one authored starter edge (18 hand-picked pairings
with a 2-beat intro scene each), then let the graph move: crossing a rung
within two weeks of your rival re-rolls the edge hotter; feeding two
partnered girls in one slot fires joint scenes with both subjects derived.
Edge state is a dimension (`rivalWith`, `pactWith` on `ctx.d`), so any
existing pool can grow edge-aware variants without new machinery.
Midgame's structural problem is that girls only relate to YOU; this
multiplies content quadratically while reusing every descriptor system
already built. Volume guard: edge scenes satisfy the same wildcard/keyed
floors, and the lint grid gains a two-girl probe axis for the joint-scene
templates.

### C2. Campus institutions (upgradeable venues as event generators)

Five institutions, each with three purchasable tiers (money + a time slot
to inaugurate), each tier changing prices, available scenes, and NPC
ecology — and each generating its own interrupts so the campus acts
between your turns:

- **Dining hall:** T1 bigger portions menu → T2 reinforced booths (unlocks
  stage-8+ group dinners; booth-fit beats move up a band) → T3 the
  standing buffet contract — all-cast eating scenes, weekly.
- **Gym:** T1 sturdier equipment → T2 "strength at any size" program
  (Serena/Brittany content; movement prose gains a proud register) → T3
  the exhibition hall for C3 events.
- **Pharmacist's clinic:** tiers track the existing Pharmacist arc docs —
  T1 consultations → T2 compounding lab (drug system surface area grows)
  → T3 the research wing that feeds C4's Researcher branch.
- **The tailor:** T1 rush alterations (wardrobe failures repairable
  same-week) → T2 made-to-measure (custom garments as gifts with fitLbs
  you choose — a deliberate strain-scheduling tool) → T3 the atelier,
  which turns size-up trips into ceremony scenes.
- **The Gainers' Society (secret):** discovered, not bought — T1 the
  invitation chain → T2 monthly "records nights" (leaderboards, C1 fuel)
  → T3 the charter, which makes the society a public institution and
  shifts campus reaction ecology game-wide.

Institutions give midgame money a destination with visible faces, extend
the existing `campusFattening`/`campusTier` machinery from ambient flag
to placeful systems, and every tier purchase is itself a scene (the
booth installation is a devotion beat, per the systems skill). Each
institution registers one interrupt family and a `locale` dimension
value, so content slots in through the standard cookbook.

### C3. Semester festivals (whole-cast setpieces on the calendar)

Four fixed setpieces a semester, visible on the planner from week 1 so
anticipation has dates: the **harvest fair** (eating games, Mary Jane's
home turf, prize foods that bank appetite buffs); the **midwinter
formal** — dress fittings the week before are the wardrobe system's
showcase episode (every girl's fit state dramatized in one montage;
alterations queue at the tailor; at least one planned failure on the
night, chosen by state, not script), then the dance itself with
relationship-gated beats; the **spring eating invitational** — brackets,
records, the C1 rivalry payoff, Reneé catering; and the **end-of-year
showcase**, which renders the entire cast at current state into one
composed sequence — group portraits by the engine's `group` context,
cross-girl comparisons via `relSize`, and public rung ceremonies for
whoever crossed that term.

Festivals snapshot the whole simulation into prose: they are where the
interleaved-arc design performs on stage, where before/after echo lands
hardest ("last fall that bench fit three of them"), and where a run's
middle gets rhythm markers players plan toward. Each festival is a beat-
family bundle (`fest.<name>.*`) swept across the full cast grid in lint —
the most demanding coverage surface in the game, on purpose: if festival
prose holds at every state, everything under it holds too. Attendance is
chosen: bringing a secret-stance girl to a public festival is a
psychology decision the reputation system (C5) notices.

### C4. Professor specialization tree (new verbs, not bigger numbers)

Three branches, three ranks each, advanced by doing (not by XP purchase):
**Nutritionist** — R1 custom meal plans (standing weekly orders per girl:
a planner automation that also carries flavor text she comments on) → R2
metabolic tuning (shift a girl's gain distribution toward her bodyType's
geography; prose visibly follows) → R3 the signature regimen, one girl at
a time, a named program with weekly check-in beats. **Researcher** — R1
studies with stipends (enroll consenting girls; data-collection beats pay
money and psych movement) → R2 published findings (campus-wide modifiers:
the paper on satiety norms nudges every NPC reaction one register) → R3
the grant, which funds an institution tier (C2) outright. **Patron** —
R1 commissions (tailor gifts, furniture upgrades as scenes) → R2 the
private table (a reserved venue slot with its own scene family and no
audience) → R3 the endowment: a campus fixture bearing consequences and,
eventually, a name the girls tease you about.

Each rank unlocks a verb that changes what a time slot IS, not a
multiplier — the test for every proposed rank is "does this add a new
kind of scene?" Picks are per-run (one branch may reach R3, a second may
reach R1, the third stays closed), so midgames diverge by build and pair
differently with cast composition: Researcher runs want Madeline and
Sophia early; Patron runs bloom with Fiona and Maya. Branch state is a
`skill` flag the engine already selects on, so content gating costs
nothing new.

### C5. Reputation & whispers (visibility as a managed resource)

A campus rumor state on two axes: **exposure** (how much is known, 0–3)
and **framing** (how it reads: discreet benefactor ↔ open legend, a
signed scale). Public beats move exposure (festival appearances, campus
scenes at high stages, institution inaugurations); your choices inside
them move framing (deflect credit, accept it, advertise). The pair is a
derived dimension (`exposureTier`, `framing`), so dialogue everywhere can
shade on it without new plumbing — the same corridor scene reads
oblivious, knowing, or reverent depending on what the campus has decided
you are.

Girls' stances filter it: secret-stance girls skip public slots with you
at high exposure (their refusal scenes say why, in voice); eager girls
seek you out — the interrupt scheduler weights petitioner events by
reputation, so at high exposure/legend framing, girls you've never fed
knock ("everyone says you're the reason the nursing student looks like
that. I want that."). Low exposure has its own rewards: private scenes
keep an intimacy register that public legend costs you, and one lategame
fork asks which you actually wanted. Whisper content is cheap and dense —
a `npc.whisper` pool keyed on exposure × framing × the biggest recent
flag, seeded through existing bystander slots. Midgame stops being a pure
resource loop and becomes about how loudly you operate, which is a
psychology dial pointed at the player for once.

## Part D — The Ascension System (endgame): 10-step implementation plan

**The fantasy:** a girl who reaches the top of the mortal ladder (stage 11
— the ladder has been labeled *ascension* since the stage table was
written) doesn't stop; she transcends. A ceremony fires, and she is reborn
in a supernatural form at 100 lbs — a mermaid, a valkyrie, a dryad — with
her memories, her voice, her relationship with you, and a brand-new gain
cycle governed by form mechanics: abilities, essence, form events, and a
final apotheosis at cycle-2 stage 11. Every existing system (appetite,
wardrobe, furniture, economy, interrupts, dialogue) keeps running,
reflavored per form. The endgame becomes: guide the whole cast through
ascension and reshape the campus into something mythic.

Each step is a shippable increment with its own verify gate, per the
repo's build discipline (lint clean → eslint → build → Dialogue Lab pass).
The Squad owns it as a ninth workstream: A4 Architect steps 1–3, A2 Psych
+ A7 Artisan on ceremony/dialogue content, A5 Editor gates every step,
A1/A3 supply stage-band prose in the new cycle, A6 owns cycle-2 stages
0–4 — the "slender again, but changed" register: her mortal early-game
content must NOT fire, because she remembers being a leviathan and has
nothing left to be uncertain about.

### Step 1 — Design lock (data before prose)

Write `docs/ASCENSION_DESIGN.md` freezing: the 18-form roster (Part E)
with every form's gain twist named in one sentence; the trigger rule and
its per-girl catalyst flags; the rebirth transaction as a field-by-field
table (what is kept, what is archived, what is reset); the essence
economy's three earn channels and typical weekly income band; ability
count (3 per girl) with the closed list of legal effect hooks; event
chain shape (3 beats + 1 interrupt family each); UI surfaces touched; and
explicit NON-goals for v1 — no form-vs-form combat, no new stage ladder,
no player ascension, no form respec. Add the Squad ownership row and a
content budget estimate (pools × floors × 18 forms) so the volume is
priced before anyone writes prose. **Verify:** doc reviewed against every
hard rule in `docs/game-skills/00`; every Part E ability maps onto an
EXISTING system hook (appetite/wardrobe/economy/interrupt/psych) — any
ability needing a brand-new system gets cut or redesigned now; two people
independently walk the rebirth table against the current save shape and
agree nothing is orphaned.

### Step 2 — State plumbing & save shape

The whole system hangs off one nullable field:

```js
student.ascension = null | {
  formId, cycle: 2, ascendedWeek, peakLbs,
  essence, abilities: { unlocked: [], cooldowns: {} },
  formFlags: {},          // per-form persistent story flags
}
```

Derivations in `textContext.js`: `formId`, `isAscended`, `essenceTier`
(0–3 ladder over essence), `auraTier` (public-manifestation intensity,
driven by essence spent in public), and `cycle`. The generalized `when`
rule makes all of these instantly selectable — no engine edits, which is
the entire reason the engine was extracted game-free. Stage derivation is
deliberately unchanged: cycle 2 reuses the 12-stage ladder on her reset
lbs, so every stage-banded system — wardrobe fit math, furniture ratings,
movement prose, weigh-ins — keeps working on day one with zero new
content, reflavored later by overlays (step 4). Save version bump with
migration (old saves get `ascension: null`); the weekly snapshot (B1)
records ascension events so Dossiers can draw the waterline. **Verify:**
node probes render a dozen existing scenes for an artificially ascended
girl with ZERO new content — nothing crashes, wildcards carry it, and the
outputs are collected as the baseline "before overlays" corpus; save
round-trips at three states including mid-ceremony; `text:lint` clean.

### Step 3 — The threshold and the ceremony

Eligibility: stage 11 + corruption tier 2 + relationship ≥ Devoted + her
personal catalyst — a per-girl flag earned by finishing her evolved-form
arc (Part E names each), so the career arc and the myth arc join into one
line instead of competing for the endgame. Girls at stage 11 WITHOUT the
catalyst get held-at-the-threshold content (a new register: enormous,
complete, waiting), which keeps the old endgame intact for players who
stop there.

Two-week buildup once eligible: the `asc.stirring.*` interrupt family —
appetite that food doesn't answer, dreams she reports in her diary, the
form bleeding through in glimpses keyed to her form (scales in the pool's
reflection, wheat in her hair that wasn't there). Then the ceremony: a
skeleton framework (`asc.ceremony.arrival` → `.gathering` → `.turn` →
`.emergence` → `.firstWords`) shared across girls, with per-girl persona
files carrying `.emergence` and `.firstWords` — this is THE persona
showcase; budget each girl's file like an ending. The rebirth transaction
executes on confirmation: store `peakLbs`, set lbs 100, archive wardrobe
(garments become **relics** — inventory items with callback scenes, not
deletions), keep every world flag and relationship value, write
`ascension`. Player consent surface: the ceremony fires only on an
explicit confirm choice framed in-fiction ("she's asking you to see this
through") — the point of no return for that girl, and declining is
content: she waits, and says so, in voice, until you're ready. **Verify:**
ceremony sweep across all 18 girls × corruption/mood grid; continuity
gate — post-rebirth renders never describe leviathan-scale body outside
designated memory/callback slots (fact `asc.reborn` asserted by the
ceremony + `forbids` on old-scale fragments); the decline path swept as
thoroughly as the accept path; lint clean.

### Step 4 — Form prose foundation (the word layer)

Form overlays on the core lexicon via `registerModuleVariants`:
`word.size`, `word.body`, `word.moveVerb`, `word.adv.*`, and the garment
pools gain formId-keyed variant groups. A mermaid at stage 6 is "heavy in
the water's hands"; a dragon's gain goes to haunch and hoard-belly and
wing-root; a dryad's reads in rings and bark-stretch. One authored
example per shape ships in the design doc so the 18 overlay sets stay
grammatically parallel:

```js
registerModuleVariants('word.moveVerb', [
  { when: { formId: 'mermaid', inWater: true }, weight: 3,
    text: ['glides', 'banks', 'soars through the lane'] },
]);
```

New beat family `asc.portrait.*` — the observation family for ascended
girls, highest render frequency of the new content, deepest coverage
required. The coverage law applies in full: form content is
weight-related, so **every form × every applicable stage band**, enforced
via a new `ascended` coverage band in `text-lint.config.js` (probes
stages 0–11 × all 18 formIds). The slender-again register: cycle-2 stages
0–4 content keys on `isAscended`, and A6's mortal early-game pools get a
`forbids`/dimension gate so reluctance content cannot leak — she is
small; she is not uncertain; the difference is the whole point of the
second cycle. **Verify:** `--coverage` ascended band ≥80% at this step,
ratcheting to 100% by step 10; stem-triple gate holds at ≤1% with 18 new
vocabularies in play; blind sample test — 10 portraits per form,
name-stripped, a reader attributes the form correctly ≥8/10.

### Step 5 — Essence and abilities framework

One resource, **essence**: earned when she gains in-form, when her
catalyst activity runs (the valkyrie's squad wins, the witch's brews are
drunk), and at cycle-2 rung crossings; the essence word itself is a pool
(`word.essence` keyed formId — brine, ichor, gleam, sap, embers) so even
the resource UI speaks in voice. Essence spends on abilities and, late,
on apotheosis progress; hoarding vs spending is a real choice because
`auraTier` (public manifestation) tracks SPENT essence, feeding C5-style
campus reaction.

Abilities are one data table, `gameData/ascension/abilities.js`:

```js
{ id: 'valk_rollcall', formId: 'valkyrie', name: 'Roll Call',
  essenceCost: 3, cooldownWeeks: 2, hook: 'psychNudge',
  params: { target: 'chosen', appetite: +2, shame: -1 },
  beat: 'asc.ability.valk_rollcall' }
```

`hook` comes from a closed set the game loop already exposes:
`appetiteMod`, `feedEvent`, `economyMod`, `interruptSpawn`, `psychNudge`,
`wardrobeEvent`, `campusMod`. Three per girl, 54 total — a content
number, not a systems number, which is what makes 54 shippable. Every
ability fire renders its beat (persona-shaded: the same hook FEELS
different from a djinn than from an angel). UI: essence meter + ability
buttons on her card; disabled states explained in-fiction ("the grove is
still drinking last week's rain"), never as gray silence. **Verify:** a
script sweeps all 54 — fire, assert the state delta matches `params`
exactly, assert the beat renders clean and names the effect; cooldown and
insufficient-essence paths render refusals in voice; lint sweep includes
every ability template.

### Step 6 — Form gain loops (the second climb feels different)

Per-form gain modifiers as data consumed by the existing weekly tick — no
second engine:

```js
FORM_GAIN_RULES.mermaid = {
  weeklyMult: 1.0, contexts: { inWater: { proseRegister: 'graceful' } },
  events: [{ id: 'tank_upgrade', atStage: [4, 7, 9], type: 'furniture' }],
  essencePerLb: 0.5,
};
```

The differentiators are twists, each mapped to a system that already
exists: the mermaid's weight is graceful in water and monumental ashore
(dual-context prose via an `inWater` dimension; her furniture ladder is
tank capacity); the dragon converts hoarded wealth to mass on a schedule
(economy inversion: money becomes food); the dryad banks growth in her
grove and blooms seasonally (planting events as gain investment); the
djinn gains per wish granted (ability-coupled); the siren per audience
(festival-coupled). Cycle-2 pacing inverts then restores the slow-burn
contract: early rungs come fast — she has done this before, and the
player has earned momentum — then rungs 8–11 gate behind essence
milestones so the finale is a project again. Numbers live in one tuning
file with design-bound comments. **Verify:** 20-week simulated runs per
form land inside the design curve bounds (script asserts min/max lbs per
week window); no form rule perturbs the mortal girls' tick (regression
run with zero ascensions matches pre-step output); interaction pass for
stacked twists (dragon hoard + patron gifts; nymph hostess-weight during
festivals); lint.

### Step 7 — Form event chains and interrupts (the content mass)

Per girl: a 3-beat signature chain (Part E) in `asc.<girl>.*` pools —
each beat a full scene with choices, not a cutscene — plus one form
interrupt family, plus callbacks that pay off pre-ascension flags: the
office chair Brittany broke is bronzed in the trophy case; the doorframe
that taught Maya now stands inside her grove's arch, grown over; relics
from step 3 get their scenes here (the mermaid keeps the swim-team jacket
dry on a hook by the tank). File layout mirrors the weigh-in exemplar:
`scenes/ascension/<girl>/index.js` + `fragments.js` + `personas.js`,
barrel-registered, Dialogue Lab SECTIONS entry per chain with its
relevant-params list. Persona lines are mined from each girl's
evolved-form arc so the ascended voice is continuous with the career
voice she built — the sphinx still talks like the girl who ran the study
groups. Volume floors apply (wildcard ≥4, keyed ≥3, persona ≥2/tier), and
chain beats satisfy stage coverage across the cycle-2 bands they can fire
in. **Verify:** `--volume` on `asc.` namespaces meets floors; every chain
rolled in the Dialogue Lab; flag-batch tuning per chain until a batch
comes back boring; ledger grep extended with form-specific bans before
writing begins, not after.

### Step 8 — Cast ecology (ascended among mortals)

Reactions: mortal girls react to an ascended girl per stance × her
`auraTier` — a secret-stance girl confides in the dryad precisely because
trees keep secrets; a rival trains harder under a valkyrie's eye; an
opposed girl finds the fertility goddess's waiting room unreasonably
calming and resents it in voice. Ascended react to each other via a
`refForm` dimension (mirroring `refArchetype`), which unlocks the
affinity-cluster scenes in Part E's notes — water court, hearth ring,
night office, spotlight, providers — the cheapest high-density ascended
content because every pairing reuses both girls' overlay vocabularies.
Group scenes: festival setpieces (C3) get ascended variants (the
invitational judged by a hearth demigoddess is a different event), and
the `group` context renders mixed processions. Campus adaptation tiers:
the shrine, the aquarium hall, the grove precinct, the hoard-vault —
institution upgrades (C2) with devotion-beat framing and per-form locale
dimensions. The knit-back mechanic: **mentorship** — an ascended girl can
