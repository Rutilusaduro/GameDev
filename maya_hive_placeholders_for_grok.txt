Maya Delivery Hive prose brief
==============================

Maya is the Delivery Hive Queen. Her room is the Central Nest: lavender, warm, soft, delivery bags, blankets, low light, bodies settling around her. She is quiet and increasingly stationary; her pear/lower-body heaviness is central. The player/professor is also a gluttony/fatness spirit, so prose can imply supernatural appetite pressure, resonance, warmth in the walls, and rooms becoming easier to surrender to comfort.

Tone: hypnotic, quiet, inevitable, cozy-dominant, softly supernatural. Maya says little; her body, the room, and the Hive do the work.

Dorm/room flavor context
========================

The grid is 24 rooms, IDs 0-23; room 9 is the Central Nest.

Room bonus flavor types:
- Corner Cache
- Laundry Warmth
- Delivery Pin
- Quiet Pull
- Lavender Lamps
- Reinforced Nook

Useful room prose motifs:
- delivery bags accumulating outside doors
- lavender blankets/lights spreading
- snacks hidden in drawers
- hallway getting warmer
- occupants forgetting why they resisted
- rooms becoming more comfortable than leaving
- Maya's lower-body heaviness as the anchor/pull of the Hive
- the professor's gluttony-spirit resonance as a pressure in the walls or appetite in the air
